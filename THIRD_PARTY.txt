STEMForge is a local orchestration and user interface layer around existing model inference projects.
Dependency versions resolved by the Windows installer may have additional licenses.

Source and documentation:
- python-audio-separator 0.47.0: https://github.com/nomadkaraoke/python-audio-separator (MIT)
- uv: https://github.com/astral-sh/uv (MIT OR Apache-2.0)
- FFmpeg: https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html (LGPL/GPL varies with binary build)
- HTDemucs: https://github.com/facebookresearch/demucs (MIT code; verify weights separately)
- MDX / RoFormer / MDX23C: inspect each model's owner and weight license separately before redistribution or commercial use.

Model weights are NOT shipped inside the archive and are downloaded by the separator engine on demand.
